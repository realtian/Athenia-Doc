Deploy once with a single command, connect your preferred AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, or Workers AI), and start chatting through the web Control UI or your favourite messaging apps — Telegram, Discord, or Slack.

## What You Get

OpenClaw on Cloudflare packages the [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) personal AI assistant into a Cloudflare Worker and Sandbox container — giving you a fully managed, always-on deployment with no infrastructure to maintain.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy to Cloudflare Workers">
    Run `npm run deploy` to build and publish the worker plus its Sandbox container to your Cloudflare account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Control UI">
    Visit your worker URL with your gateway token — `https://your-worker.workers.dev/?token=YOUR_TOKEN` — to access the web chat interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pair Your Devices">
    Approve new browsers or chat-platform connections through the Admin UI at `/_admin/` before they can interact with your assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Chatting">
    Use the Control UI, or message your assistant through Telegram, Discord, or Slack after connecting the optional integrations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The first request after a cold start may take 1–2 minutes while the container initialises. Subsequent requests are fast.
</Note>
